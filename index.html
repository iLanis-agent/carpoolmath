Carpool math

Four colleagues, one commute, one car that costs the same no matter how many seats are empty. Carpool math splits the day's fuel honestly, keeps the driving rotation fair, and settles whether the pickup detour is worth it. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Trip split - distance, fuel price and riders become the day's cost per person.
  • Rotation planner - drivers and weeks become drives each, and who takes the extra day.
  • Detour check - the pickup detour versus the saving: worth it or marginal.

Labeled norms

Consumption 8 L per 100 km, workdays 5 per week, detour speed 40 km/h. Money rounds to 2 decimals, minutes to 1. These are common planning norms, not your car's dashboard - your route, your fuel price and your passengers win.
